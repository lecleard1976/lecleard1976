## Manufacturing Industry Experience

•	BES supports more than 1,750 clients and 160,000 trading partners globally and processes more than 3 billion transactions per year.

•	BES has more than 25 years of EDI and trading community integration experience; and more than 20 years of experience providing e-commerce-related services to the manufacturing industry, serving clients in the automotive, high technology, and consumer products sectors. In fact, hundreds of clients in the manufacturing industry accounts for 25% of our business exchange revenue. Our services are focused on helping manufacturers succeed at making products faster, better, and at a lower cost to remain competitive in their marketplace.

•	DXC Business Exchange Services streamlines transaction processing for manufacturers who can capitalize on competitively priced services that are tailored to their specific needs providing these advantages:
   * Low capital investment
   * Straightforward, usage-based pricing — pay a single fee per transaction
   * Volume discounts offered
   * Subscription-based or fixed-fee pricing also available
   * Predictable, transparent charges
   * Rapid implementation and migration
   * Scalability to accommodate seasonality and business growth
   * Predictable service levels
   * Disaster recovery

•	Outsourcing the management of business transactions to DXC BES lets manufacturers focus on core activities instead of spending time and money on technical issues. It also delivers the following benefits:
   * Improves trading partner relationships
   * Optimizes cash flow
   * Increases transaction accuracy
   * Ensures timely transaction delivery
   * Enhances flexibility
   * Ensures industry compliance

•	BES provides a suite of offerings that address manufacturing industry e-commerce needs that can be matched and scaled, including:
   * Connectivity via value-added networks, Internet, telephone networks, and other communications
   * Translation (standard and proprietary formats)
   * Media conversion (between ASCII and fax/paper)
   * Extended archiving
   * Trading community management
   * Trading partner supply chain software and services
