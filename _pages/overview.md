## Our Team
The Global SME team is a global team, our goal is to enable DXC to deliver and excel in the global marketplace through guidance on processes, tools and accelerators to enable delivery excellence for our clients in a consistent and flexible manner. TIPS (Tool Finder), along with Let's Connect, Automation Launchpad and SME Central are the main components of our ecosystem. These are complemented by user communities such as AgileDXC and DevOpsDXC.

For our content refer to [SME Central](https://github.dxc.com/pages/AET/EDGE-Central/Pages/Ops_Home.html).
