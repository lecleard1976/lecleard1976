## Automotive Industry Experience

•	BES supports more than 1,750 clients and 135,000 trading partners globally

•	Processes more than 3 billion transactions per year 

•	Supports more than 2,000 EDI translations (maps) for our automotive clients, including all common translations from/to Odette, VDA, EDIFACT, ANSI X.12, XML and others

•	BES roots are in automotive EDI – we started in the 1980s with large EDI programs for GM (US) and GM Europe (Opel-Vauxhall-Saab)  

•	Automotive customers include GM, Volkswagen, SKF, NSK, Gates, Lear, Thomas Built Buses, Allison Transmission, Ally Bank and Affinia and many others 

•	Supports active EDI links to all major automotive OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers including:
   * _**OEMs:** GM, GM Europe, Holden, Ford, Chrysler, Peugeot, Citroen, Renault, Volkswagen, Audi, Porsche, Seat, Skoda, Volvo, Jaguar Land Rover, Nissan, Toyota, BMW, Ferrari, Fiat, Honda, Hyundai, Iveco, Isuzu, Man, MCC, Mitsubishi, Scania, DAF, KIA, Maserati, Caterpillar, CNH, Piaggio …._ 
   * _**Tier 1 Suppliers:** Delphi, Visteon, Lear, Bosch, Johnson Controls, Dana, Detroit Diesel, Eaton, Karmann, Textron, Denso, Magna, Magneti, Michelin, Motorola, NSK, PPG, Tenneco, Gates, ThyssenKrupp, Tyco, Valeo, Continental AG, ZF Friedrichshafen and thousands more_

•	The automotive industry faces significant challenges ranging from globalization and market volatility to excess capacity and declining margins, value chain complexities, competitive pressures, and changing consumer demands. DXC helps automotive companies meet those challenges by transforming how they manage their product and technology portfolios, interact with car buyers, achieve market differentiation, and accelerate downstream expansion.

•	DXC has an excellent track record of delivering managed B2B services to automotive industry clients for the last 25 years. Specifically, we work with a number of Tier I and Tier II automotive customers, helping them with their EDI solutions in a managed service mode. We have the experience and understanding of the business and technology requirements of the automotive supply chain.

•	DXC is deeply embedded in the automotive ecosystem and offers a solid understanding of the complete value chain. Our products and services form the IT technology foundation at leading automotive companies globally. DXC strengthens that portfolio with billions of annual R&D investments. We leverage the capabilities and strategic relationships to help automotive companies drive growth, overcome complexities, differentiate, and achieve organizational fitness.

•	BES has more than 25 years of EDI and trading community integration experience; and more than 20 years of experience providing e-commerce-related services to the manufacturing industry, serving clients in the automotive, high technology, and consumer products sectors. In fact, the manufacturing industry accounts for some 25% of our business exchange revenue. Our services are focused on helping manufacturers succeed at making products faster, better, and at a lower cost to remain competitive in their marketplace.
