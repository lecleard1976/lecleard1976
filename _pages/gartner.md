## Insights
__Gartner__, the industry and research advisory firm, defines integration Brokerage (IB) as a category of IT outsourcing for application and data integration projects that includes initial
integration project implementations and recurring managed services. IB is often called a B2B or electronic data interchange (EDI) managed service because of its prominent use in e-commerce.
Commonly available, IB is also used for cloud service integration, mobile app integration, internal application-to-application (A2A) integration, digital business initiatives and the Internet of Things (IoT) integration.

__Last BES Vendor Briefing with Gartner__ with [Benoit J. Lheureux](https://www.gartner.com/analyst/12543)


## What Gartner published about BES
  __2016:__  "HPE" is listed as one of 22 representative vendors in Gartner's ["Market Guide for Integration Brokerage"](https://www.gartner.com/doc/3551218/market-guide-integration-brokerage) with BES as HPE's IB offering.


    "Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) Business Exchanges Services (BES) is a mature IB offering with service delivery and a large portfolio of clients worldwide. It serves many
     industries and geographies, giving HPE a broad vertical market footprint. HPE delivers IB in both standalone and embedded offering (e.g. within broader SI projects).
     HPE offers flexible cloud- or on-premises delivery options and can implement projects using technology from HPE, IBM, OpenText, SEEBURGER, Axway, SAP, TradeExpress and
     Edifecs, with tools for accelerated client migrations. Typical use cases include supply chain integration, MFT (including App-to-App and ad-hoc MFT use cases), e-invoicing,
     B2B visibility and trading partner on-boarding/management."

  __2015:__   "HP" is listed as one of 22 representative vendors in Gartner's ["Market Guide for Integration Brokerage"](https://www.gartner.com/doc/3133417/market-guide-integration-brokerage) with BES as HP's IB offering.

  __2014:__   "HP Enterprise Services" is ranked as a Challenger in Gartner's ["Magic Quadrant for Integration Brokerage"](https://www.gartner.com/doc/2701218/magic-quadrant-integration-brokerage) with BES as the fulfilling group. 

  __2012:__   "HP" is ranked as #2 in vendor market share in Gartner's ["Competitive Landscape - Integration Brokerage"](https://www.gartner.com/doc/2250015/competitive-landscape-integration-brokerage) 2011 study but HP had no other vendor details included.
