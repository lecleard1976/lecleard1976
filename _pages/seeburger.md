## Go-To Team in BES for [SEEBURGER](https://seeburger.com) Integration Solutions
In close partnership with SEEBURGER, we provide tailored EDI and B2B solutions for our customers and for DXC itself. Our team works directly with SEEBURGER’s Development team to influence current and future products. We have extensive experience in migrating legacy EDI and B2B environments to state-of-the-art SEEBURGER products as well as in operating these environments efficiently.

Our team consists of EDI and B2B experts from around the world with 20+ years of EDI/B2B experience and 15+ years of SEEBURGER experience on average. As part of the global DXC BES organization, we have access to 200+ further integration experts. We are equipped to solve your most complex challenges around:

  •	Migration of your legacy B2B Integration system to a state-of-the-art environment based on SEEBURGER products

  •	Working with your trading partners to ensure a smooth transition for them, utilizing a self-service approach

  •	Assessing your mapping environment and setting up a new structure to allow for streamlined, re-usable and well-documented mapping repository that can remain that way long-term

  •	Architecting and configuring your new application environment on-premise or in the cloud to ensure performance, stability and efficiency
  
  •	Meeting Enterprise level redundancy needs with High Availability (>99.99%) and Disaster Recovery solutions

  •	Operating and supporting the application environment efficiently or helping you to support the environment yourself 

  •	Direct access to the BES VAN and its 160,000+ connected enterprises 



## SEEBURGER Product Portfolio and associated Services
Contact us with your requirements and we can help you build the right solution and services based on the SEEBURGER Product Portfolio. Some of the most popular building blocks are:

  •	Central EDI and B2B Gateway based on SEEBURGER Business Integration Suite (BIS) on-premise or in the cloud

  •	Partner Management and Self-Service with the SEEBURGER Community Management Application (CMA)

  •	Business Visibility with customized SEEBURGER Message Tracking

  •	Change Management Automation and control with SEEBURGER MTP tools

  •	Managed Manual File Transfer - including large files - with SEEBURGER File Exchange Services (FXS)
