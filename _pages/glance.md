## Insights
•	There’s another way to connect business partners beyond traditional electronic data interchange (EDI).

•	DXC Technology can help you better manage your business-to-business trading community through a single interface.

•	Fortune 500 companies, such as GM and P&G, benefit from our global B2B expertise.

Do you need better B2B — all around the world? Business Exchange Services (BES) knows how to achieve it. Our single interface eliminates problems inherent in multiple systems across different business partners.
BES delivers proven B2B e-commerce connectivity, integration, and process management solutions. We help you manage the flow of information and data translations with your entire global trading partner community.
Many companies that conduct business via traditional EDI struggle to connect with their trading partners. The community often uses a variety of nonintegrated EDI platforms and networks, resulting in costly, slow, and risky information transfers. Noncompliant transactions — brought on by changing legislation and industry standards — only make matters worse. The situation is unmanageable, characterized by redundant and error-ridden data, inconsistent service levels, and limited visibility into business process performance. Even worse, the ensuing chaos can lead to loss of customers.

## Streamlined business processes
The world’s largest non-tire rubber manufacturer, needed fast, easy EDI connections to modernize processes, handle multiple standards, and reduce expenses. That’s when the manufacturer turned to BES. The single-platform business exchange solution enabled the seamless flow of information and payments. Regardless of the communications channel or information format, neither the trading partner nor manufacturer had to install complex or expensive EDI equipment. And the BES help desk serves as an initial contact point for operational issues. Errors are now tracked quickly and solved efficiently.

The manufacturer reduced manual processes and established new e-communications connections more easily, expediting the incorporation of new trading partners. These realized efficiencies enabled the manufacturer to refocus resources on new, mission-critical projects rather than IT.
