Microservices is an application architecture where a solution is built as a series of small and narrowly focused modular services, i.e., mini applications, which together address the business needs of a customer.  Each microservice  should be designed to serve a single logical business domain function that can woven together with other  microservices in order to perform a business task.

Each microservice should be self-contained, in that it encapsulates all the functionality and data layer required to fulfill its specific business function. Microservices are loosely coupled with clear dependencies, wherein each service must have a well defined interface to allow the microservices to interact seamlessly with one other in order to fulfill more complex business transactions.

With Microservices there is no requirement that all services must be created  using a single platform, a specific coding language, or even the same development team.

The benefits of microservices are many, but they also come with their own set of security and management challenges. Make sure your project has an approved plan and skills in place to address these challenges before adopting Microservices for your solution.
